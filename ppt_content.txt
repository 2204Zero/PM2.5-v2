Fire hotspot detection was performed using DBSCAN clustering on VIIRS fire observations.

The algorithm identified 53 hotspot clusters across India and classified 262 points as noise.

The largest hotspot cluster contained 3624 fire detections, indicating a major concentration of fire activity.

DBSCAN successfully separated dense fire regions from isolated fire events and provided spatial hotspot identification without requiring a predefined number of clusters.
